## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are Hedera x402 bounty judges evaluating a live demo, followed by developers building autonomous agents. They need to understand the product in seconds, then watch an agent discover a paid market-data resource, receive HTTP 402, authorize a bounded micropayment, retry, and use the result in a portfolio decision. The interface must also make the resulting Hedera testnet proof easy to inspect.

### Brand Personality
Precise, credible, quietly ambitious. The product should create confidence and clarity rather than crypto spectacle. Voice is concise and factual, with payment and network states explained in plain language.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light-first financial operations console with editorial restraint: warm off-white surfaces, deep ink/navy text, muted slate structure, green reserved for positive or settled states, amber for payment-required and review states, and Hedera violet as a small identity accent. Avoid heavy dark themes, neon glows, loud gradients, glassmorphism, generic dashboard card grids, and decorative crypto imagery. Use purposeful asymmetry and a visible event rail to make the 402-to-settlement story memorable.

### Design Principles
1. Make the autonomous payment loop legible at a glance: intent, 402 challenge, signed payment, 200 response, portfolio action.
2. Use color semantically and sparingly; layout, type, and whitespace carry hierarchy.
3. Put verifiable proof next to every claim: amount, payer, payee, transaction ID, and HashScan link.
4. Keep demo-critical actions on one screen and preserve them on mobile.
5. Respect reduced motion, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, and honest testnet/mock-data labeling.
